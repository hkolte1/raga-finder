# raga-finder


**Purpose** 

If you know the Raga: what are the notes of the raga ? So you can tell the accompanist ? Or if you are the accompanist and someone tells you that the song is from this raga, you can quickly check what the raga notes

If you know the song, and as an accompanist or singer, you need to let them know what raga it is so they can get a general sense of the notes of the raga and the particular song.

If you know the notes you are playing but cant remember the raga: Click on the notes in the panel and it will pop up the closest matches to a possible raga.

If you want to know the corresponding Carnatic raga or Melakarta raga: The results of any of the above flows will show the corresponding ragas in the Carnatic system

**Raga finder results:**

**Inputs:**
- Click the notes on the keyboard and click search OR_
- Type in Raga name and search

**Results**

- Thaat (there is a separate tab that also gets populated in addition to just the name)
- Jati
- Aaroha - notes, as well as a Play button that plays the notes 
- Avroha - notes, as well as a Play button that plays the notes
- Vadi
- Samwadi
- Closest Carnatic raga - may not always be an exact match
- Closest Melakarata raga - again not a 1 to 1 mapping with Hindustani raga
- Additional details
- Few songs from that Raga

Type in Notes results

**Song finder results:**

**Inputs:**
Type in words from a song

**Results**

All songs that are in the database that have a match
Corresponding Raga. You can then Clear the results and search using Raga name

**Raga Ring**

Purpose:  

To show ragas in the Hindustani system as they relate to each other and the thaats
- You can click on the Raga ring to show all the 10 Hindustani Thaats
- Click on ragas on the wheel that are in the same Thaat to compare
 Has all the raga results, as from the Ragafinder
- Shows the transitions from one Thaat to another

**Melakarta Wheel**


Purpose:  To show ragas in the Carnatic system as they relate to each other and the thaats
You can click on any Melakarta ragas (72) and it will show the

Carnatic swara notations
Equivalent Hindustani swara notations
The Melakarta chakra
And if found - the equivalent Hindustani raga
